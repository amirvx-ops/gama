SELECT CASE WHEN product IN ('פנסיה', 'פנסיה משלימה') THEN 'פנסיה' WHEN product IN ('גמל', 'גמל להשקעה') THEN 'גמל' WHEN product = 'השתלמות' THEN 'השתלמות' WHEN product = 'ריסק' THEN 'ריסק' WHEN product IN ('בריאות', 'מחלות קשות') THEN 'בריאות' ELSE 'אחר' END AS product_group, COUNT(*) AS count FROM gama_policies WHERE (COALESCE(NULLIF({{YearFilter.selectedOptionValue}}, ''), EXTRACT(YEAR FROM date)::text) IS NOT DISTINCT FROM EXTRACT(YEAR FROM date)::text) AND (COALESCE(NULLIF({{MonthFilter.selectedOptionValue}}, ''), EXTRACT(MONTH FROM date)::text) IS NOT DISTINCT FROM EXTRACT(MONTH FROM date)::text) AND (COALESCE(NULLIF({{CompanyFilter.selectedOptionValue}}, ''), company) IS NOT DISTINCT FROM company) AND (COALESCE(NULLIF({{ActionFilter.selectedOptionValue}}, ''), action) IS NOT DISTINCT FROM action) GROUP BY product_group ORDER BY count DESC
